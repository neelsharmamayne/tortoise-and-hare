The Tortoise
and the Hare

A Living Storybook

scroll to begin

Tortoise was friendly and quiet — he did everything slowly. Hare was flashy and boastful — he did everything quickly. Hare loved to tease Tortoise about being so slow.

One afternoon, Hare followed Tortoise to the store, teasing him along the way. “You’re so slow, I could beat you hopping backwards!” Tortoise’s friends — Frog, Raccoon, and Rooster — had heard enough. “Race him,” they urged. “We think you can win.”

Tortoise had only two weeks to get ready. Rooster coached him at the gym. Raccoon cooked him healthy meals. Frog jogged with him every morning. By race day, Tortoise was ready.

Animals from all over came to watch. Rooster read the rules and Raccoon sounded the gong. “Runners, take your mark — get set — GO!” Hare bolted out of sight before Tortoise had taken his first step.

Hare was so far ahead that he stopped at Bear’s house for something cool to drink. He sipped lemonade and rested. “Hare, there goes Tortoise!” said Bear, peering out the window. “What?!” yelled Hare, running out the door.

Hare passed Tortoise easily, then stopped at Mouse’s house for a snack. As he munched on crackers and cheese, Mouse yelled, “Is that Tortoise I see out the window?” “I’m not worried about that slowpoke,” said Hare. “I’ve passed him twice already.”

Hare passed Tortoise for a third time. Now far ahead, he saw a pond and sat beneath a shady tree. The snacks had made him sleepy. He was so sure he would win — he took a nap in the soft grass, dreaming of victory.

While Hare slept, Tortoise kept walking — one steady step after another. He passed the pond, he passed the tree, and he passed the sleeping Hare without making a sound.

Suddenly, Hare woke to the sound of cheering. Tortoise was two steps from the finish line! Hare scrambled to catch up, but it was too late. Tortoise crossed the line just ahead of the tornado of fur that was Hare.

Tortoise smiled as his friends carried him on their shoulders. He had learned an important lesson:

“Hard work and perseverance bring reward.”
